# POL-003: Infection Control Manual
version: 2
## 1. Hand Hygiene
1.1 Staff must perform hand hygiene before and after every patient contact.
1.2 Hand hygiene compliance must be audited monthly, with a target of at least 95%.
## 2. Isolation
2.1 Patients with airborne infections must be placed in a negative-pressure room.
2.2 Visitors are not permitted in isolation rooms except with infection control approval.
2.3 Isolation precautions may be discontinued after two consecutive negative tests, 24 hours apart.
## 3. Reporting
3.1 Outbreaks of three or more linked cases must be reported to public health within 24 hours.
3.2 Hospital-acquired infection rates above 5% require a formal corrective action plan.
