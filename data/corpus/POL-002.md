# POL-002: Patient Discharge Procedure
version: 1
## 1. Discharge Criteria
1.1 A patient may be discharged only after a physician signs the discharge order.
1.2 Discharge planning must begin within 24 hours of admission.
1.3 Patients with an unresolved fall risk must not be discharged without a home safety review.
## 2. Follow-up
2.1 A follow-up call must be made within 48 hours of discharge.
2.2 Readmission within 30 days must be reviewed by the quality committee.
2.3 Discharge paperwork may be completed by a nurse when the physician is unavailable.
