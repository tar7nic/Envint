# POL-007: Patient Records and Privacy
version: 1
## 1. Retention and Consent
1.1 Patient records must be retained for at least 10 years after last treatment.
1.2 Records of minors must be retained until the patient reaches age 25 or for 10 years, whichever is longer.
1.3 Patient information must not be shared with third parties without written consent.
1.4 Information may be disclosed without consent when required by law.
## 2. Access Control
2.1 Unauthorised access to records must be reported to the privacy officer within 72 hours.
2.2 Access logs must be reviewed quarterly.
2.3 Staff must not access records of patients not under their care.
