# POL-010: Visitor Policy
version: 1
## 1. Visiting Rules
1.1 General visiting hours are 10:00 to 20:00.
1.2 No more than two visitors are permitted at a bedside at one time.
1.3 Children under 12 may visit only with an accompanying adult.
1.4 In the intensive care unit, visiting is limited to immediate family.
1.5 Visitors with respiratory symptoms must not enter patient areas.
## 2. Exceptions
2.1 End-of-life patients may receive visitors outside general visiting hours.
2.2 Visitors must wear masks in all patient areas during a declared outbreak.
