# POL-008: Equipment Maintenance
version: 1
## 1. Testing Schedule
1.1 Defibrillators must be tested daily.
1.2 Ventilators must be serviced every 6 months.
1.3 Infusion pumps must be calibrated annually.
1.4 Equipment failing a safety test must be tagged out of service immediately.
## 2. Return to Service
2.1 Tagged equipment may return to service only after biomedical engineering approval.
2.2 Maintenance records must be kept for 5 years.
