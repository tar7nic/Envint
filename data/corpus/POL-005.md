# POL-005: Staffing and Shift Rules
version: 1
## 1. Nursing Hours and Ratios
1.1 Nurses must not work more than 12 consecutive hours.
1.2 Nurses must have at least 10 hours of rest between shifts.
1.3 The nurse-to-patient ratio in general wards must not exceed 1:6.
1.4 The nurse-to-patient ratio in intensive care must not exceed 1:2.
1.5 Overtime beyond 12 hours may be approved by the department head in emergencies only.
## 2. Agency Staff
2.1 Agency staff must complete orientation before their first unsupervised shift.
