# POL-001: Cold Chain Storage Policy
version: 1
## 1. Medication Storage
1.1 Vaccines must be stored between 2°C and 8°C at all times.
1.2 Vaccines must not be stored above 8°C for more than 30 minutes.
1.3 Room-temperature medications may be stored up to 25°C.
1.4 Temperature logs must be reviewed every 24 hours by the pharmacy lead.
## 2. Excursions
2.1 Any temperature excursion above 8°C must be reported within 2 hours.
2.2 Vaccines exposed to temperatures below 0°C must be discarded.
