# POL-009: Blood Transfusion Safety
version: 1
## 1. Storage and Administration
1.1 Two staff members must verify patient identity at the bedside before transfusion.
1.2 Blood products must be transfused within 4 hours of leaving controlled storage.
1.3 Red blood cells must be stored at 2°C to 6°C.
1.4 Platelets must be stored at 20°C to 24°C with continuous agitation.
1.5 Platelets must not be refrigerated.
## 2. Monitoring
2.1 Transfusion reactions must be reported to the blood bank immediately.
2.2 Patients must be observed every 15 minutes during the first hour of transfusion.
