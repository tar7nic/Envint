# POL-006: Emergency Department Triage
version: 1
## 1. Triage Targets
1.1 Triage must be completed within 10 minutes of patient arrival.
1.2 Category 1 resuscitation patients must be seen immediately.
1.3 Category 3 patients should be seen within 30 minutes.
1.4 Category 5 patients may wait up to 120 minutes.
## 2. Escalation
2.1 Patients waiting longer than 4 hours must be escalated to the duty manager.
2.2 Ambulance handover must be completed within 15 minutes.
