# POL-004: Medication Administration
version: 1
## 1. High-Risk Medications
1.1 Two nurses must independently verify high-risk medications before administration.
1.2 Controlled substances must be stored in a double-locked cabinet.
1.3 Controlled substance counts must be reconciled at every shift change.
1.4 Verbal medication orders may be accepted only in emergencies and must be countersigned within 24 hours.
1.5 Verbal orders are not permitted for chemotherapy drugs under any circumstances.
## 2. Errors
2.1 Medication errors must be reported within 12 hours using the incident system.
